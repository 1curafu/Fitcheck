### Critical — temperature zeroes the pairings the table itself rates 5

`lib/generator/styling/temperature.ts:37` — `(share - 0.5) / 0.2` has no floor and no notion of count. With 2 temperature-bearing colours the only reachable shares are 0.5 and 1.0, so 70/30 is *unrepresentable* and any warm+cool pair is forced to the worst possible value, 0, costing the full 0.30.
